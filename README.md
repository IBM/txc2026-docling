# Docling at IBM TechXchange 2026

Materials for the [Docling](https://github.com/docling-project/docling) sessions
at **IBM TechXchange 2026** — hands-on labs and demos for turning documents into
data your applications and agents can actually use.

Each lab lives in its own directory with its own README.

> [!NOTE]
> **The event is over, and this repository is archived.** Thanks to everyone
> who joined us in Atlanta! The lab materials stay here for reference, but
> they are no longer maintained. The workshop environments we provided
> (VMs, credentials, instances) are gone, but you don't need them for most of
> the labs: you can follow along on your own machine with your own
> [Docling for IBM watsonx](https://www.ibm.com/products/docling) instance
> (a free trial is available).
>
> To keep going, several of the scenarios from these sessions are now part of
> the [Docling for IBM watsonx examples](https://github.com/IBM/docling-ibm-examples),
> with step-by-step tutorials in the
> [Docling for IBM watsonx documentation](https://developer.dcls.saas.ibm.com/):
>
> - [Quick start](https://github.com/IBM/docling-ibm-examples/tree/main/01-quickstart):
>   convert a document with the API and the Python SDK
>   ([docs](https://developer.dcls.saas.ibm.com/quick-start))
> - [RAG in a notebook](https://github.com/IBM/docling-ibm-examples/tree/main/02-rag-notebook):
>   convert, chunk, embed, and answer with watsonx.ai
>   ([tutorial](https://developer.dcls.saas.ibm.com/tutorials/notebook-rag))
> - [Vector database ingestion](https://github.com/IBM/docling-ibm-examples/tree/main/03-vector-db-ingestion):
>   from public URLs into an AstraDB collection in one batch request
>   ([tutorial](https://developer.dcls.saas.ibm.com/tutorials/vector-db-ingestion))
> - [Docling in IBM Bob](https://github.com/IBM/docling-ibm-examples/tree/main/04-bob-agent-skill):
>   the agent skill used in LAB-1499
>   ([tutorial](https://developer.dcls.saas.ibm.com/tutorials/bob-agent-skill))
> - [Confluent Cloud](https://github.com/IBM/docling-ibm-examples/tree/main/05-confluent-cloud):
>   stream document chunks into Confluent Cloud and clean them with Flink SQL,
>   as in LAB-2775
>   ([tutorial](https://developer.dcls.saas.ibm.com/tutorials/confluent-cloud))
>
> Questions or ideas? Open an issue in the
> [examples repository](https://github.com/IBM/docling-ibm-examples/issues).

These were our sessions in Atlanta:

### 🧪 LAB-1499 — Hands-On with Docling for IBM watsonx

Convert, extract, and build your own document-powered app. Turn a bundle of
PDFs, Word documents, and EPUBs into a filled compliance form — with IBM Bob or
a Python notebook, no GPU required.
